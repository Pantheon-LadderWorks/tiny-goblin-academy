## Purpose and Boundary

H5.101 auditions the accepted H5.100 neutral pantry and H5.100C stylized-fantasy pantry on eight code-authored neutral specimen families. It compares material recipes rather than raw source squares and preserves identical geometry across candidates. It does not build the Potion Sorter room, begin the SceneRig, add a particle system, author shaders, alter gameplay, or select runtime assets.

## Audition Method

The reproducible Canvas laboratory renders:

- timber beams;
- a masonry arch;
- repeated conveyor slats;
- iron rails and brackets;
- a gear and valve with constrained Metal008 brass;
- parchment labels and recipe cards with live typography;
- code-authored glass bottles carrying red, blue, and green liquid classifications;
- source-only FX helper sprites.

Every structural specimen is evaluated under neutral inspection light and warm amber mechanism light. The evidence set also includes a combined coherence board at 1920×1080 and the Academy's 1024×640 minimum desktop contract.

## Brass Finding

The realistic Metal008 brass does provide the requested contrast and focal pop. On the shared gear silhouette, the constrained brass hub makes the interaction target immediately legible against painted iron. When the same texture expands into a broad gear face, its photographic surface response takes over the object and weakens the stylized room language. The provisional law is therefore:

> Realistic brass may punctuate authored machinery; it may not become the machinery's broad visual identity.

## Rejected and Deferred Treatments

- broad realistic brass coverage;
- photographic timber as the primary room identity;
- rust-heavy realistic iron across broad machinery;
- grime or surface imperfections used as a repeating fill;
- external glass texture acquisition without a demonstrated code-authored gap.

These decisions do not reject the source pantry items themselves. They constrain how the sources may participate in a later Potion Sorter SceneRig.
